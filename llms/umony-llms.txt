# Umony

> Umony is a London-based communications compliance platform, founded in 2017 and backed by Seedcamp, that captures, records, and archives employee voice calls, SMS, WhatsApp, and Microsoft Teams conversations at the telecom-network level for banks and other regulated financial institutions. Its products include the Umony App (BYOD softphone), Umony Mobile (eSIM carrier-level capture), Umony for Teams, Umony Archive (AI risk-scoring across 14 compliance categories), and Umony Review (AI-powered supervision). Umony holds BSI-certified ISO 27001:2022, ISO 42001:2023, and ISO 22301:2019 certifications. Umony publishes no public developer API, documentation portal, or SDKs as of 2026-07-21.
